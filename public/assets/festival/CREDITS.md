# Festival scenery — sources and reuse

## Kenney Nature Kit 2.1 (CC0)

Author: Kenney, https://kenney.nl
Primary source and license: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
Downloaded from the official asset-page link:
https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/nature-kit/37ac38a37b-1677698939/kenney_nature-kit.zip

The seven selected GLBs are unchanged authored models from the pack: tree_detailed, tree_detailed_fall, tree_pineTallA_detailed, rock_largeA, rock_tallB, mushroom_redGroup, mushroom_redTall. The original LICENSE.txt accompanies them. The pack grants CC0 use in personal, educational and commercial projects, modification and redistribution; attribution is voluntary and included here.

These models use embedded material colors, not external textures, so no atlas is required. Runtime adaptation scales/places models, bakes source material colors into vertex colors and merges static scenery into one geometry to reduce draw calls. Road proximity checks keep scenic objects away from the racing surface.

## Original Palm Prix geometry

The rotating festival wheel, its supports and upright cabins, striped racing balloons and pastel crystal clusters are original game geometry. No Nintendo or other proprietary extracted assets are used in this scenery.
