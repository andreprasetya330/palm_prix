## Retained original Palm Dart and garage art

The Palm Dart is an original Palm Prix kart. Body, helmet, geometric visor, face, tires, tread, and livery were modeled for this project. No Nintendo assets are used.

Openly licensed parts may be downloaded while modeling (CC0 or CC-BY only). Credit them here. The exported kart stays an original Palm Prix asset.
